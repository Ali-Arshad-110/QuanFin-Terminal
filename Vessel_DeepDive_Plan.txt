================================================================================
SECTION 1: DATA SOURCE & ARCHITECTURE
================================================================================
Data is fetched exclusively from AISStream.io (Official v0 Satellite Relay).
The architecture uses a high-performance WebSocket Proxy (Backend) to bridge
raw satellite signals to our Frontend.

[Source Breakdown]
- Provider: stream.aisstream.io (Satellite + Terrestrial Nodes)
- Frequency:
  * Positional Data (Sog/Cog/Lat/Lng): Every 2-10 Seconds.
  * Static Voyage Data (Destination/ETA/Draft): Every 6 Minutes.
- Protocol: JSON via WSS (Secure WebSocket).

================================================================================
SECTION 2: WHY THIS DATA MATTERS (THE "SO WHAT?")
================================================================================

1. DRAFT (The Profit Signal):
   Use: Draft is the distance between the waterline and the bottom of the hull.
   Value: A high Draft means the ship is HEAVILY LOADED. This helps us track 
          exactly when massive amounts of commodities (Oil, Coal, Iron) are 
          entering or leaving India before official reports are out.

2. DESTINATION & ETA:
   Use: Tracking the "Route Confidence".
   Value: Foreseeing port congestion. If 15 tankers are headed to Jamnagar 
          simultaneously with close ETAs, we can predict a supply-chain bottleneck
          and market price volatility.

3. VESSEL TYPE (Market Segmentation):
   Use: Tanker vs. Cargo vs. LCG Carrier.
   Value: Allows institutional users to filter only for "Energy Trade" or 
          "Consumer Goods" depending on their trading focus.

4. NAVIGATIONAL STATUS (Logistical Efficiency):
   Use: Distinguishing between 'Underway' and 'At Anchor'.
   Value: Ships "At Anchor" for long periods signal poor port efficiency or 
          low demand at that destination.
